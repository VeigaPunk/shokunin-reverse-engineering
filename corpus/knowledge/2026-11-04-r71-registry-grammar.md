## Operability: the registry drift census reads its own block

`tools/round-51/census3.py` required exactly seven `|`-split cells per registry row and exited 2 with “registry row must have seven cells” as soon as rounds 69/70 appended their rows: four round-69 rows carry a bare `|` inside the pinned-value cell (eight cells) and six round-70 rows omit the owner cell (six cells). The census had been unevaluable since `616a5f2f` (round-70 M70-7) — a drift gate that dies on the first malformed row reports nothing about the rest.

`tools/round-71/registry-census.py` declares grammar v2: split on **unescaped** pipes and unescape `\|` (S258’s rule); the first four cells are tool · purpose · verdict map · self-test; the last cell is the owner; the second-to-last is the write scope; every cell between the self-test and those two is the pinned-value path, re-joined with `|`. A row is conformant when it has at least six cells, a backtick-delimited normalized path that exists at the measured revision, and a non-empty re-joined pinned cell; a row with more than seven cells is conformant only for the four declared round-69 paths (an eight-cell row), so a new over-count row is `cells>7`, and a blank purpose, verdict, self-test, write scope or owner is `blank-cell`. The six round-70 rows reconstruct no pinned cell (the `pinned-empty` class); the round-71 record appends the owner cell they omitted, disclosed old→new in `LOG.md`, and the four round-69 rows are never rewritten.

The selftest (14 cases) exercises the pure parser and the outer CLI: a clean seven-cell row, the round-69 eight-cell shape, an escaped pipe inside a cell, the six-cell defect, a short row, a new eight-cell row, a blank-cell row, an absent path, a duplicate, three marker failures, and the CLI exits with their finding codes. ## Coverage: the route’s children are observed

The round-70 route passed the four-key allowlist to the packer but let the REA `node`, `readelf` and `git` children inherit the caller’s environment (`SentinelScope` advisory 2). Round 71 routes every child of the route through `build_child_env(run_root)` and adds `--child-env-log`: a launcher records its own `os.environ` (the block `execvp` passes to the child) and argv into a JSONL line before exec, and the receipt summarises the observation (`child_env_observed`: records, keys, children, `all_allowlisted`, `home_under_run`, `tmpdir_under_run`, `canary_present`). The capture is threaded through the REA analyze/decompile children and the route’s `git` closure as well as the packer, list, readelf and control calls, so the log carries the analysis children, not only version probes.

The launcher is an interpreter and adds `LC_CTYPE` to its own environment when it coerces the C locale. That one artifact is excluded by construction: the route hands the launcher `PYTHONCOERCECLOCALE=0` and the launcher pops that marker before both the observation and the exec. The arm’s claim is therefore “the route passed exactly HOME/LANG/PATH/TMPDIR” — not “the runtime adds nothing”, which would be false. Containment is `os.path.commonpath`, not a string prefix: a sibling directory named `<run_root>-outside` is not a descendant.

`seam-probe.py` A8 parses the raw JSONL itself (never the builder’s returned dict); A9 starts three battery runs and three route preflights concurrently on one `--work` parent, uses run-relative log names so each log is written under its own `run_root`, and requires distinct work dirs, distinct run roots, exact key sets and descendant containment.

## Calibration: a pre-committed run binding, and the lying-field battery

`--challenge <32 lowercase hex>` with `--nonce-commitment <sha256>` makes the receipt carry `run_nonce` and `run_binding = sha256(nonce + ":" + instrument.sha256 + ":" + sorted packed pins)`. `--check-receipt --nonce-commitment` requires sha256(`run_nonce`) to equal the commitment, the binding to re-derive, a non-empty `run_root` and an observed-env summary consistent with the allowlist. The landing nonce’s sha256 is committed at `l1/pre/nonce-commitment.txt` before the run; the raw nonce-bearing receipt stays in scratch and only a redacted summary, the checker’s output and the raw child log are filed.

This is **replay and transplant rejection, not cryptographic authenticity**: the commitment is public; an actor who reads the genuine receipt can recompute a receipt with the same nonce and binding. What it establishes is that a receipt cannot be transplanted from another run (its nonce hashes to a different commitment) and that a field-consistent receipt whose binding was altered is rejected. No claim here covers a signer or a trusted timestamp.

The forged-receipt battery carries 18 forged rows: the ten base-world shapes (round-69 material, base reader at `5cc845ca`, superseded reader at `7c155617`), the two landing-world identity shapes, and the six lying-field classes the round-70 supersession bound but never exercised: `unpacked_counts`, `packed_sha256`, `section_count`/`upx_list_rc`, `runs` argv (a dropped `--best`), `refusal`, and `format`. Each is cut from the round-70 landing receipt and relabelled to the round-70 first-cut world (`4737a542`): the first-cut reader accepts it (the defect) and the superseded reader rejects it naming the class.

## Limits

- The observed-env arm covers the route’s own children on this host; it does not cover REA’s internal children, the packer’s own subprocesses, or a hostile caller who edits the launcher. The checker mode’s own `readelf`/`git` reads are outside this claim.
- The binding’s guarantee is replay/transplant rejection on the committed commitment and the withheld nonce; it is not a signature and does not authenticate the host.
- The launcher’s one declared artifact exclusion (`PYTHONCOERCECLOCALE`) is the only key it removes; nothing else is filtered.
- The carrier set, packer pin and REA pin are the round-70 tables; nothing here claims another packer, carrier class or REA version.
- The census reads the SKILL registry block only; a malformed row in another table is out of scope and named by no instrument.
- The six round-70 rows are repaired in the record commit; the four round-69 rows stay byte-identical and their multicell shape is a recorded grammar fact, not a defect. A GFM-faithful padding reader is a documented false green, not the census’s contract.
