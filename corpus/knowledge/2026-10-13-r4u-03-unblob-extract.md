`~/.local/share/uv/tools/unblob/bin/unblob`, unblob 26.6.4 via uv) made

## Summary
`extract-firmware` is live and honest: unblob 26.6.4 under
`REA_UNBLOB_COMMAND` gives typed, per-chunk results — full carve where
extractors exist (`.pkg.tar.zst`: zstd→tar→gzip, 100% identification,
verified pacman metadata), clean empty output on opaque data
(`ucodes_tu10x.bin`), and identify-plus-preserve where an extractor is
missing (AppImage: ELF carved, 127.5 MB squashfs chunk written to disk
with a typed `ExtractorDependencyNotFoundReport`, 99.89% identification
ratio). The single practically-relevant gap is `sasquatch` — it is the
sole blocker for squashfs recursion and binwalk 3.1.0 hits the identical
wall at the identical offset, so this is a host toolchain gap, not an
unblob gap. Two mitigations verified: (a) `7z` is installed and reads
unblob's carved squashfs directly — a working carve-then-7z pipeline
today; (b) installing sasquatch would make unblob recurse natively.
unblob's coverage on this corpus strictly dominates binwalk's (binwalk
missed the entire zstd-wrapped pkg; on the AppImage it left nothing
carved while unblob preserved the chunk). Caveats for downstream lanes:
output dirs must be absent before each run; evidence_ids are per-run,
not per-content; `--max-output-bytes` tops out at 512 MiB so >256 MiB
extractions need the flag explicitly.
