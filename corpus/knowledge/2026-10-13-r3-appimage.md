## Candidate: `inspect_artifact` classifies the AppImage as a monolithic ELF — one node, zero edges, no squashfs members, no archive treatment

The single occurrence is `entry_kind:"file"`, `artifact_kind:"executable"`, `artifact_format:"elf"`, `declared_size:134055925` (whole file, squashfs included), `hash_status:"verified"`. The recorded limitation says it outright: **"Artifact has no child container entries."** `extract_artifact` agrees: refuses in 0.86 s with `unsupported_target` / `"Artifact format has no extraction reader: elf"` (non-retryable). The offered `next_probe` is `open_binary` ("Bind one deep native provider"), i.e., the ELF path — there is no container branch.

## Summary

- **Ground truth:** payload offset 188392 = flush after ELF section-header table; squashfs v4.0 LE zlib, 164 inodes, 133,724,992 B; then an opaque ~141 KiB high-entropy trailer (candidate: signature/update block — unverified).
