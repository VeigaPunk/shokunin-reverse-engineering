# Historical UTF-8 boundaries

François Yergeau’s [RFC 2279](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc2279.txt), January 1998, §2, allows sequences through six octets. His [RFC 3629](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc3629.txt), November 2003, §§3–4 and 12, narrows the range and requires protection against invalid sequences; Unicode remains authoritative.

The useful distinction is between a historical encoding definition and evidence of what a particular implementation accepts. Neither document alone identifies a current decoder’s behavior.

These are official publication texts. Internet Society permissions cover conditional copying and commentary, with document alteration restrictions, not unrestricted reuse or training permission.
