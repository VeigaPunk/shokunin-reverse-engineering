# Kaidoku: source coordinates and blocks

UNIX International, [DWARF 2.0.0](https://dwarfstd.org/doc/dwarf-2.0.0.pdf), 27 July 1993 Industry Review Draft, §6.2 introduction–6.2.2: encoded line tables associate machine instructions with source coordinates. Reuse requires preserved notices and respects name/publicity restrictions.

DWARF Committee, [DWARF 4](https://dwarfstd.org/doc/DWARF4.pdf), 10 June 2010, §6.2 introduction–6.2.2 and §6.2.5.3 item 4: discriminator labels distinguish blocks sharing file, line and column. Their arbitrary values do not establish execution order. This concerns static representation, not execution reconstruction or a complete decoder. GFDL 1.3, no invariant sections or cover texts.
