## Proposed rule #16

- **Content identity does not re-locate a perishable pointer.** Treat a receipt/hash, a semantic anchor, and a `path:line` locator as separate evidence. A receipt digest binds the captured bytes, not their current position; a successful `path:line` check proves only that the addressed line contains the expected token, not that it is in the intended stanza or unique. For an operator-mutable file, resolve the current line from a stable semantic key, check the selected bytes against the receipt, and re-key the locator after movement. If any part is missing, call only the supported scope verified; do not turn a pointer-level pass into a whole-document or whole-gate pass.

This extends, rather than replaces, rule #11 (`SKILL.md:196-217`), which already rejects bare line-number citations into operator-mutable files. The proposed addition states what each check proves and prevents a line-level success from being overstated.
