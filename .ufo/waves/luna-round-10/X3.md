## CLAIM
If another arc is run, four controls should be entry/closure gates: exact seat reconciliation, tested census instruments, isolated/nonessential `/tmp`, and a named independent SHONEN verifier.

## METHOD
Read-only review of `LOG.md`, `SCORECARD.md`, `SHONEN.md`, and rounds 2, 4–9 reports; recommendations below are scoped to the evidence cited.

## FINDINGS
- **Dispatch counting:** Generate a frozen expected roster of unique seat IDs (64 per lane), then reconcile dispatched and received IDs per lane after each batch/backfill; do not close until missing and duplicate IDs are zero. Explicit undercounts recur in rounds 2, 4, 5, and 6 (`LOG.md:660-663,722-724,757-760,789-794`); round 6 records 127 distinct seats and a duplicate EX7 despite the self-check (`.ufo/reports/luna-round-6/kaidoku.md:47-52`). Round 7 reports a 59+5 lane-B backfill (`.ufo/reports/luna-round-7/kaidoku.md:3-4`). The arc instruction identifies five affected rounds; this review did not independently locate the fifth incident.
- **Census instruments:** Parse structured fields, and require positive, negative, and malformed-input fixtures before a matcher can return PASS. A comma-normalization check was unsatisfiable and a wording detector matched retained text; these were correctly adjudicated as instrument defects, not content verdicts (`SCORECARD.md:576-589`). Preserve that distinction and retest repaired matchers.
- **`/tmp` policy:** Treat it as isolated scratch, never a prerequisite; namespace by round/lane/seat, copy/hash any required durable receipt into the authorized evidence location, and never clean shared scratch without authorization. Round 9 found a shared-name collision (`LOG.md:296-298`); round 6 left ~1.2 GB because other sessions may share `/tmp` (`LOG.md:808-809`). Cold-cache remains untested (`SCORECARD.md:591-599`).
- **SHONEN verifier:** Recruit and name a non-author verifier outside the dispatched seats before kickoff; assign each gate and required evidence explicitly. All four boxes remain `[~]` with no identified outside verifier (`SHONEN.md:5-12`); round 6 confirms cross-lane replay seats do not satisfy the outside-verifier bar (`.ufo/reports/luna-round-6/kaidoku.md:69-74`).
