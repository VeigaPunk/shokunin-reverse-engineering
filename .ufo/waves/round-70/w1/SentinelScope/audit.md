## Advisory

- **`tools/round-70/forged-receipts.py:320-321` — selftest scratch may escape caller work.** `--selftest` accepts no `--work` (argument handling at lines 383-388) and chooses `work or os.environ.get("TMPDIR") or tempfile.gettempdir()` as the `mkdtemp` parent. Thus a selftest without `--work` writes its fixture under caller-selected `TMPDIR` or the platform temp location (commonly `/tmp`), not a seat-owned scratch root; normal completion removes it in `finally` at line 335. Smallest remediation: require an explicit work root for selftest, or create and clean an owned subdirectory under a required caller scratch root.
- **`tools/round-70/packed-elf.py:249-260, 263-273, 526-527` — non-packer children inherit the caller environment.** The packer calls explicitly pass `build_child_env` (`:321-335`, `:563-590`), which has the four-key `HOME`, `LANG`, `PATH`, `TMPDIR` allowlist. By contrast, `rea()` calls `run()` without `env` (`:259-260`), so REA's Node process inherits the full parent environment; the `node --version` call does too (`:527`). `readelf` and local git subprocesses also omit `env` (e.g. `:358-359`, `:568`, `:590`, `:493-494`). This is not evidence of secret exposure, but a caller's credentials or other environment values are available to those children. Smallest remediation: pass a deliberate child environment to these calls too, if the REA contract supports it; otherwise document the narrower trust boundary and ensure the runner does not carry secrets.
