## Not run / limits

- Not run on the real lane at any revision: the counter is not committed there, so a lane run would exit 2 `counter_not_frozen`; the fixture F/G chain is the only evidence. The landing text, the real S150–S153 rows and the real C1 knowledge entry are untested against the atoms.
- B8's snapshot and the S138 `N files` check were exercised only with the synthetic `corpus/evidence/round-50/c2/s138-census-listing.txt`.
- The B10 stand-in inside `--selftest` is a two-key fixture script; the full draft `selftest-r49-c2.py` is exercised by the `green-battery`/`green-plain` runs (B10 shells it) and by `r49-selftest`.
- Disclosed limits of the counter itself: consistency of the record against committed bytes, not judgment of prose; `identified`-style asserted numbers are not used here; the registry check is row presence + cell shape + meaning words, not that any entry command runs; tier text and live-page fidelity are not re-derived (the sha-pinned snapshots are self-attested); the counter cannot bind which commit the operator passes as `--freeze`.
- Host load average was ≈ 70 during the runs; `user` CPU times in the raw `time` output are inflated by contention and are not a property of the counter.
