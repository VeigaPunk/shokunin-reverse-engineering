## Verdict

**The P50-C1 predicate, as drafted, has surviving shortcuts; it is not shortcut-proof.** Every receipt-level shortcut (relabelled BE/LE receipts, pre-freeze receipts, fabricated envelopes, copied round-36/41/44/49 runs) is stopped only by the counter-time rerun (`projection_ok`, plus `oracle_hits_ok`). That rerun is not bound to the REA package, unblob or sasquatch it names: `make_real_runner` executes whatever `npx -y rea-agents@6.5.0` resolves through the inherited environment, and `--pkg65` feeds only the evidence-id step. Reproduced twice (once here, once in round 49 W2). Three further holes sit in the drift walk and counter-bytes binding, which the round-41 base lacks and round 49 partly repaired. Fix the rerun binding, the drift walk, the counter-bytes binding and the oracle provenance before the freeze. Otherwise the freeze will score an honest-looking 4/4 that no clause ties to REA running on vendor bytes.
