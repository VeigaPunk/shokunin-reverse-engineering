## The device-accounting law

[observation] **The law, stated before the freeze (the claim text, wave-1 amendments F3/F5/F8 applied):** the extraction producer (REA 6.5.0 `extract-firmware`, handler `squashfs_v4_le`, unblob 26.6.4 → sasquatch `-le`) materialises regular files and directories and re-creates symlinks (with a `MaliciousSymlinkRemoved` diagnostic when it refuses one), but it creates **no device nodes, fifos or sockets** — an unprivileged extraction cannot `mknod` — and it emits **no gap record for them**: a char-device/fifo/socket oracle entry carries **no disposition at all**. Because the law is that no gap record exists for such an entry, the frozen rule admits **no** gap kind for those kinds (`allowed` for a device kind is the empty list), so a gap landing on a device row is a violation of the law, not a tolerated state — a future extractor that starts emitting a device gap turns the rule **red** (fail closed).

[observation] **The law's primary grounding is the measurement, not the source rows.** Over the four committed round-50 envelopes, every char-device row of the independent census carries no extraction gap and no published path — **28 of 28 rows** (1 + 1 + 13 + 13): `law-device-disposition` receipt, 2026-10-30, exit 0, stdout sha256 `474854813592bbc5…` (`python3 -I -B .ufo/waves/round-51/w1/ScribeEntryC1/law-device-disposition.py --lane <LANE>`). The only gap kind that occurs at all in those envelopes is `symlink`. The unsquashfs/squashfs-tools row opened this round grounds the **census** (how a device inode is created and what a reader does when it cannot create one), not the extractor's silence — the two are different claims and the entry keeps them apart.

[observation] **The law's width, stated honestly.** For the four measured LE AVM carriers the law is measured for **char-device** rows only (28 of 28). The generalisation to the other device-node kinds (block device, fifo, socket) rests on the unprivileged-`mknod` ground, and **the corpus does not exercise it**: no fifo, socket or block-device row exists in any of the four oracle tables (the kinds present are `symlink`, `dir`, `char-device` only — `census-kinds` receipt, 2026-10-30, exit 0, stdout sha256 `7a666895d951d373…`).

[observation] **The law is a statement about the entries the extractor reached.** The receipts do not name the path a `depth-limited` gap stopped at (`relative_path` is null), and a device-only subtree (e.g. `dev/pts/`, which holds no regular file) that was never descended into cannot be distinguished from one that was reached and correctly produced no gap. The v2 counter records the `depth-limited` gap's `input_path` and `continuation_id` in its receipt (`depth_gap_recorded`) so the limit stays visible.

[observation] **Cross-claim obligation.** R51-C2's `SKILL.md` rule block 27 supersedes or scopes block 26's "`symlink` and `special` both count" phrasing, so that the two claims land no contradiction about device nodes. Where block 26 says a `special` entry counts as an unpublished entry, block 27 scopes it: a device row that the producer never materialises carries no disposition, which is a different statement from a `special` entry the producer did materialise.

## Limits (the limits of R51-C1, restated)

1. [observation] **Vendor bytes are fetched, never committed.** AVM's licence permits one personal backup copy only (S249). An image AVM replaces makes the chain unfetchable (the four are long-standing releases of their models, not a guarantee), and the counter-time rerun is unrunnable without a `--carrier-cache`. The committed receipts and oracle tables are the durable evidence.
2. [observation] **The extraction tree is never committed.** REA's extraction output lives under the scratch carrier cache and is not part of the lane; what is committed is the receipt (the envelope, the gaps, the diagnostics) and the frozen oracle / non-regular tables. The receipt is the only record of the tree.
3. [observation] **Availability is not holding.** The claim's subject is four pinned vendor images that the publisher may withdraw or replace, and the round-51 data run's reproducibility depends on the chain being fetchable at that moment; the lane holds hashes, listings and receipts, never the bytes. Nothing in this entry should be read as a statement that the four images will remain available.
4. [observation] The AVM marker is invisible to unblob and re-stampable: unblob 26.6.4 tests `mkfs_time == bytes_used` only inside its **big-endian** v4 branch (S245 :48), so an AVM-marker LE member and a standard LE member take one handler and one argv. The claim is "REA publishes every oracle file of these four pinned vendor members under the law-derived accounting rule", not "REA or unblob recognises AVM".
5. [observation] Four carriers, one vendor, xz only, depth 1 — this is not "every LE AVM image". The carriers are re-measured, not new coverage; the promotion rests on the completed 4/4 measurement under the corrected law (claims amendment F1, option b), and the anti-replay waiver for those four pins is explicit and visible in the receipt.
6. [observation] REA's 120 s `provider_timeout` is a host-load property. The rerun repeats up to 3 times on a timeout only; a persistent timeout is exit 2, never a pass. The round-50 `g7581` ENOENT rerun failure is recorded above and is not a pass.
7. [observation] The oracle rows for symlinks compare path and kind, not `link_target` (unblob rewrites targets), and the set of removed `MaliciousSymlinkRemoved` links depends on readdir order — so the rule is set-agnostic, and the round-50 entry's stronger "observation" label on the order dependence is not inherited here (see **What moved, and only what moved**).
8. [observation] **The law's width.** Measured for char-device rows only (28 of 28). The block-device / fifo / socket part of the law rests on the unprivileged-`mknod` ground and is **not exercised** by the corpus — no such row exists in any of the four oracle tables.
9. [observation] **The law's reached scope.** A device-only subtree that was never descended into cannot be distinguished from one that was reached and correctly produced no gap; the `depth-limited` gap's `input_path` / `continuation_id` are recorded (`depth_gap_recorded`) so the limit stays visible.
10. [observation] The documentation bindings are presence checks: a `PROVINCE.md` Core row naming `squashfs_v4_le` and the AVM marker, a `Round-51 frontier note` line, the entry itself, `corpus/README.md`'s coverage-map row and the SOURCES rows' `used-by` cells. A citation-present binding is not a semantic proof of the sentence it binds.
11. [observation] The pre-freeze controls are **rule-scoped**: they compare a named rule's disposition verdict over committed bytes. They do not and cannot exercise the byte-dependent clauses of the full predicate, and no control result may be read as a data-run result.
