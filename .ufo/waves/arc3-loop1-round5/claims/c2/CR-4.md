## Method

Read the three dated arc3 EVM entries plus the Ghidra entry’s round-2 and arc3-r4 errata. First-hand checks used Python `hashlib` on carrier bytes, `Crypto.Hash.keccak` from the wave-4 venv to derive selectors from ABI canonical signatures, and direct byte parsing of the locally stored bytecode / sealed `raw_result.functions`. For each probe, decoded `normalized_result.bytecode.hex`, recomputed its length and SHA-256, and compared it with the stored `.bin` or decoded `.hex` when present. No target was executed and REA was not re-run; results below are an audit of the retained evidence, not a claim of a new REA observation.
