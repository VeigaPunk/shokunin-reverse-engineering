# CR-3 — C2 arc3 corpus correctness audit

**Verdict:** the selector counts, pinned source/runtime identities, byte sizes and dispatcher observations for the retained arc3 R1, R3 and R4 artifacts reproduce, subject to the corrections and qualification below. One R1 carrier-size/digest sentence needs an append-only wording correction; the existing R1 creation/PUSH4 erratum is supported. The cross-file Core-status wording is unresolved. No corpus or lane files were changed.

## Method and limits

Re-ran `python3 .ufo/waves/arc3-loop1-round5/claims/c2/CR-3/evidence/rederive.py` from the `kaidoku-shokunin` root. It recomputes SHA-256 and decoded lengths from retained source/carrier bytes; compares normalized probe selector sets with pre-probe planted sets (and ABI/signature-derived sets where applicable); checks decoded bytecode hashes; and decodes the relevant dispatcher instructions and R1/R3 creation-code sites. Its local Keccak helper is checked against the canonical empty-input vector. This is a static audit of retained envelopes and bytes, not a fresh REA invocation, compilation, chain lookup, or runtime execution. Selector/inference results remain tool observations, not proof of deployed behavior.

Separately ran `.ufo/waves/arc3-loop1-round5/claims/c2/CR-3/evidence/r4-creation-sweep.py` from the lane root; its stdout is retained in `.ufo/waves/arc3-loop1-round5/claims/c2/CR-3/evidence/r4-creation-sweep.out`. The sweep recursively enumerates R4 C1 JSON files with `creation` in the basename and checks both `normalized_result.functions` and `raw_result.functions`: **23 envelopes; all 23 are 0/0**. Directly listed the local Ghidra `Ghidra/Features` directory: **34 entries**.
