A kill rule covers every gate its instrument asserts, and a green verdict exists only through a verified receipt.** <!-- kill-rule-gate-coverage v1 --> The kill-rule-gate-coverage class is the one where a frozen kill rule asserts a gate that its own counting commands never reach, so a mutant that removes the gate survives a green verdict that measured something else. Round 95 measured two instances of it and each is carried into this block. M95-h is the mutation finding: the frozen round-95 kill rule killed only 5 of the 10 round-95 mutants because its checks never called the counter's `main()` and never ran the probe, so the receipt write's fsync removal (M2), the forced self-verification (M3), the removed fail-closed exit (M4), the removed revision binding (M6) and the probe's hard-coded verdict (M10) all survived it (`.ufo/waves/round-95/w1/MutationC1.md`; `.ufo/waves/round-95/w1/MutationC1/mutants.md`; the same rule re-measured with a receipt at `.ufo/waves/round-96/pre/c1-legacy-rule-at-base.json`: killed 5 of 10, survivors exactly M2, M3, M4, M6 and M10). M95-g is the receipt half of the same class: six fields the receipt claimed were unbound, the self-verification was skippable, and a public run without `--json-out` exited 0 on a met-True verdict having written and verified no receipt at all (`.ufo/waves/round-95/w1/CriticC1.md`; `.ufo/waves/round-96/pre/c1-v5-admits-unbound-at-base.json`: accepted 5 of 6, one bare `KeyError` crash, nojson exit 0). The round-96 instruments answer the class from both sides: `tools/round-96/kill-rule-v2.py` adds to the frozen rule's own set the four checks the mutation finding named — a `main()`-level negative control, an instrumented fsync count, a probe receipt-content check and the no-json-out refusal — and `tools/round-96/stop-boundary-repair-battery-v6.py` binds every field the receipt claims (head, repair pin, counter pin, verdict pin, downward met direction, typed counts) and makes the receipt mandatory on every exit, while `tools/round-96/symlink-probe-v2.py` writes its own receipt through the hardened writer.
