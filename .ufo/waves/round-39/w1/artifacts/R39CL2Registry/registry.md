## Notes (read with every row)

1. **Crash-collapses-to-`failed` is lane-wide.** In all five Python counters an uncaught exception
   exits `1`, the code reserved for `failed`, so a traceback is never distinguishable from a
   measured miss. The round-37 counter is the live instance (row 3); the r35/r38 counters share the
   shape but have not crashed in the runs retained here.
2. **Three QUALITY-bound gates still have no self-test.** `freeze-drift.py` (QUALITY 79/80),
   `intended-doc.py` (QUALITY 81) and `registry-immutable.sh` are gates by criterion, not by
   conformance: `PROTOCOL.md` §Gate standard's self-test clause is unmet at this revision. Their
   rows name the round's seat (`R39CL2Selftests`) and its flag spelling (`--selftest`); for the
   bash script no spelling exists yet and one must be defined by that seat. Until then the honest
   reading is "gate by criterion, unregistered-conformance", which is what R38-CL2 asked to end.
3. **`registry-immutable.sh`'s admission binding is indirect.** QUALITY rows 79/80/81 name
   `freeze-drift.py` and `intended-doc.py`; nothing in `corpus/QUALITY.md` names
   `registry-immutable.sh`. Its binding comes from CORPUS rule 2 (append-only SOURCES) plus the
   round-38 spot-check's classification. It is registered as a gate on that basis, and the gap is
   listed under "not verified".
4. **The round-37 counter is a probe, not a gate, and R37-C1's verdict must not rest on it.** It
   fails before scoring (row 3). Its selftest is double-broken: argparse makes it unreachable, and
   the branch crashes on its own fixture because `make_fixture` never creates `corpus/knowledge`.
   The instrument also writes fixtures through a spliced block inside `measure()`, so a repair is
   a rewrite, not a one-line flag.
5. **"Selftest green" here is capability, not measurement** (§Gate standard "Two evidences"). Rows
   6 and 8 read their fixtures from the working tree, so a moving worktree changes the self-test
   input; only the counters' own on-revision runs are comparable to a cited commit.
6. **No row has a digest-pinned `expected.json`.** Expectations live in the scripts, in the frozen
   claims/carriers blobs and in the selftest case tables — the same limitation the round-36
   registry records as its limit 7.
7. **Shared dependency, out of scope.** `tools/round-27/xpin-attribute.mjs` is read by rows 3, 6 and
   12; it is pinned only by the freeze commit. Other host dependencies: `node` (xpin, rows 6/12),
   `npx`/`rea-agents@6.5.0` (rows 4, 7), `jq` (row 4), `git`, `python3`.
