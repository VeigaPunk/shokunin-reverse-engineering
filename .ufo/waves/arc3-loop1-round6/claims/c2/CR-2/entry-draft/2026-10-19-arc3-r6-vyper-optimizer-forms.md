- **Observation (Form C — `-O codesize`; the third Vyper dispatch form here, and the first perfect-hash selector map in this corpus — Form A is also a jump-table dispatcher):** a two-level perfect-hash dispatcher with **no selector-shaped `PUSH4` in the dispatch region**. Grammar measured on both carriers:
  1. `selector = CALLDATALOAD(0) >> 224`.
  2. `bucket = selector & (nbuckets-1)` (the 1-bucket build omits the `AND`; equivalent for nbuckets=1), then copy the 5-byte level-1 entry at `table_base + 5*bucket`. Layout `[multiplier:2][record_base:2][count:1]`.
  3. `slot = ((multiplier * selector) >> 24) % count`; copy the 7-byte record at `record_base + 7*slot`. Layout `[selector:4][dest:2][guard:1]`.
  4. Require `selector == record >> 24`; jump to `(record >> 8) & 0xffff`; the guard byte drives a calldata-size/callvalue check (`… PUSH1 fe AND CALLDATASIZE LT CALLVALUE DUP3 PUSH1 01 AND MUL OR PUSH2 <revert> JUMPI`).
  CarrierV: `table_base 0x537`, **2 buckets** (`selector & 1`); bucket 0 `000a054105` → multiplier `0x000a`, count 5, record base `0x0541`; bucket 1 `0a1f05640a` → multiplier `0x0a1f`, count 10, record base `0x0564`. Ex09Vault: `table_base 0x206`, **1 bucket**; `0092020b07` → multiplier `0x0092`, count 7, record base `0x020b`. The hash-slot identity holds for **all 22 records** (15 + 7) and every record's `dest` is a JUMPDEST (22/22). Bucket count is compiler-chosen, not fixed: 2 at K=15 and 1 at K=7 in this sample, so a recogniser must classify the prologue's bucket-selection shape (presence/absence of the `AND`+`MUL` pair before the first `CODECOPY`), not assume 2.
