Reading: selector identity is exact on every leg; mutability is an upstream inference and is
optimizer-form-scoped here. On K8 (all eight ABI functions view) every form reads payable (0/8).
On Ex05Vault the default form agrees 3/7 (delta payable, epsilon view, eta payable);
--no-optimize and -O codesize agree 2/7 (delta, eta) because every function reads payable.
The r4 arg-taking-payable class reproduces on the default form (4/4 arg-taking read payable,
including the pure `beta`), but the r4 zero-arg-agreement half does not generalize intact:
zeta() (ABI nonpayable, zero-arg) reads view on the default form and payable on the non-default forms.
