## 1. Verdict

**THE POSTURE IS HONEST; THE LABELS ARE NOT. Three named defects, one of which re-publishes a
number the arc's own audit refuted.**

- **D1 — axis mislabel.** `AXES.md:16` defines adjacency as *"Cross-domain awareness — evidence
  belonging to another domain reported, not absorbed"*. A provider/substrate census is
  same-lane, same-domain metadata, and `PROTOCOL.md:15-16` makes provider identity explicitly
  substrate-neutral (*"every rule here is substrate-neutral"*). C3 is therefore **not an
  adjacency move**. Landing it under the label `adjacency — C3` invites exactly the absorption
  the framing forbids: it reads as expansion-trend credit (`AXES.md:21-22`), and it implies the
  hyper lane is another *domain* when the protocol says it is not. The honest label is a
  **fleet-axis recording of a substrate substitution** (the `SCORECARD.md:376-380` form:
  recorded as a fleet-axis improvement, *"owned axes explicitly unchanged — no owned proxy is
  re-scored"*).
- **D2 — baseline mislabel.** "The devin-lane waves" are not the waves adjacent to r10. The
  loop-1 record: r1 mixed (18 opencode-go first attempt + 46 devin replacements), r2/r3/r4
  `devin/deepseek-v4-1-flash-max`, **r5 `openai-codex/gpt-6-luna-max` (60)**, **r6
  `xai-oauth/grok-4.7-fast` (128)**, **r7/r8 `zai/glm-5.3-flash` (24, 20)**, **r9
  `openai-codex/gpt-6-luna-max` (24)**. Only r1-r4 are devin. `E9.md:103-125` folds R5 (codex)
  into its "Devin-lane delivery baseline" table; `E15.md:310` writes *"C3's census baseline is
  the devin-lane waves"* into the LOG skeleton. `E14.md:355-364` does keep r5-r9 as
  `(context)` — that part is honest, and should be the form used everywhere.
- **D3 — the census draft re-publishes refuted numbers.** `E14.md:343-350` prints r2/r3/r4 as
  *"64 seats / 60 delivered / 93.8%"* and a *"devin-lane subtotal **256 / 240 / 93.75%**"*.
  Round 5's own audit declares that record **FALSE** (`SCORECARD.md:1016-1017`: *"the items
  arrays carried 60; all 60 completed every wave; the '4 non-completions' … were fictional"*;
  `LOG.md:1183-1186`). `E9.md:20-25` and `E19.md:214` get it right (`60/60` each → fully-devin
  **180/180**). An operator-facing census may not carry a superseded count; two of the three
  census drafts already avoid it.

**And the comparison itself carries no signal.** Delivery is **100% in every wave r1-r9** once
the correction is applied — zero variance across lanes. The round's quality gates (census v10,
citation-drift probe, command A) are deterministic scripts over repo text, provider-independent
by construction. Convergence varies with the claims *declared* per round, not with the lane.
So the honest census verdict is *"no lane-differentiated signal on this record"* — a stronger
and more useful operator statement than a comparison table with a 93.75% cell in it.

**What is honest and should be kept as-is:**

1. `E3.md:277` names the r10-mid vs r6-mid delta precisely (systemd cgroup wrapper with
   `MemoryMax=9G`/`MemorySwapMax=1G`; `npx -y` vs bare `node`) and explicitly refuses the
   attribution (*"the parent's adjudication, not this seat's claim"*).
2. `E3.md:303` / `E4.md:13,16,289-294` — the one **like-for-like** lane pair this round
   (r10-big ↔ r7-big, identical recipe): byte-identical 5204 B envelope, `ev_a4aa9b0d…` equal.
   Independently re-verified by this seat (§2.5).
3. `E19.md:251-254` — the bounds block (*"lane and model suffix both differ — this measures the
   substitution, not a controlled experiment"*; r1 mixed-lane exclusion rule).
4. `LOG.md:1273-1281` — the substitution record itself (mandated by the substitution rule) is
   complete and correct.

**Process defect, not a framing defect:** the seat that carries the `C3` label delivered a
*containment watch plan* (`C3.md:1,8`), not the census. The census is split across `E9`
(delivery), `E14` (LOG/SCORECARD draft), `E19` (table skeleton), `E15` (records skeleton) — so
the claim has no single owning artifact, which is how D3 survived to the draft stage.
