## 3. Census method (what the parent runs at integration close)

Four steps, in this order — the order matters because step 1 is what failed in
rounds 2-4:

1. **Items, not intent.** Record `len(items)` from the dispatch call itself.
   If it is not 64, the declared count is wrong and the census denominator is
   the items length (lesson rule 1, `…-wave-dispatch-counting.md:21-24`).
2. **Roster set-difference.** Set-diff the 64 reconstructed roster names
   against the `.md` basenames in the wave dir. Reconstruct from
   `receipts/roster-frozen.txt:3`, not from another round's filenames.
3. **Sizes + structural markers.** The probe in §2.5, run on the wave dir.
4. **Live legs.** Terminal class per leg from `systemctl --user show` + journal;
   a leg in flight at close is *not verified* (never a kill, never a
   shortfall). Both legs are terminal as of this seat's close.

Report the result as **delivered/dispatched**, and separately as
**roster-covered/64**. Never print a 64-denominator shortfall without a
set-difference to back it.

## 4. Convergence vs the devin waves' patterns

**F1 — The "devin delivery pattern" is a counting artifact, and it does not
transfer as a provider signal.** Three waves, identical missing set, zero
extras, and a round-5 audit that re-derived the items array at 60
(`LOG.md:1183-1190`). A provider-shaped failure would not select the same four
IDs three times; a roster-construction slip does. Consequence for the
operator's substitution decision: **the hyper lane cannot be judged by "more
than 60/64"** — that comparison is against a superseded number. The honest
comparison is 60/60 vs (r10 delivered)/64-with-its-items-length-stated.

**F2 — Report size is a weak, lane-correlated proxy; the devin band is
21-27 KB mean at 60 seats.** Rounds 2→3→4 rise monotonically (21.0 → 26.0 →
27.0 KB mean) within a single provider, which tracks the wave prompt's growing
evidence demands, not the provider. Other lanes sit lower at smaller seat
counts (r6 10.4 KB at 128, r7 13.4 KB at 24, r8 11.0 KB at 20, r9 5.6 KB at
24). Inference, not measurement: **size tracks seat count and prompt shape
more than provider.** Round 10's 17.5 KB mean at 64 seats is inside the devin
band's lower half — no size signal in either direction.

**F3 — Structural completeness is near-saturated on every lane, with a
seat-count-dependent tail.** Every wave has 100% `VERDICT` + labels; the gaps
are shell-command lines (r6 112/128, r8 19/20, r9 19/24) and one fence (r9
23/24). The devin lane has **zero** structural gaps across 180 reports; the
r10 snapshot matches that (31/31 on all four markers).

**F4 — Record-form convergence (the defect class, not the count).** The devin
rounds' *record* defect was "declared 64, dispatched 60, recorded 4

"the same defect class the luna arc recorded five times"
