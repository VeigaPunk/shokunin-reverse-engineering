## 1. Verdict

**SPLIT — and the split is itself the finding: the wave's controlled-experiment
discipline held on the big leg and broke on the mid leg, at the recipe-transcription
step, before either leg launched.**

- **`arc2-r10-big` vs r7 — FAIR CONTROLLED RERUN. 9 of 10 axes identical, the tenth
  non-material, and the output is byte-identical.** Target bytes, request argv, all
  six unit env keys (including the disabled-analyzer name string), the JVM
  pre/post-script chain, `MemoryMax`/`MemorySwapMax`, heap, deadline, provider/JDK/node
  pins and custody all match r7. The only env drift is `TMPDIR=/var/tmp/arc2-l10r10-big`
  (r7: `/var/tmp/arc2-l7r7-pe`), which r7's own recheck line leaves open ("disk
  `TMPDIR`", `.ufo/waves/arc2-loop1-round7/E4.md:118`). Result: sealed envelope **5204 B,
  sha256 `a77ebee8794fb5951cc5bc79f46000e1fdd29c3ed09542d30c791a89941c218a` — `cmp`
  clean against the r7 receipt, `diff` empty**, `evidence_id ev_a4aa9b0d…` re-derived,
  census identical. The leg is a *valid* experiment on the question it was asked.
- **`arc2-r10-mid` vs r6 — NOT A RERUN; A CAP-CONFOUNDED RE-TEST.** Four drifts, one of
  them fatal and absent from r6's record: `MemoryMax=9663676416` (+1G swap cap) was
  **added**; the launcher changed (`npx -y rea-agents@6.5.0` instead of r6's
  `node …/rea-agents/scripts/rea.mjs`); **`--format json` was dropped**; the outer
  `timeout -k 60 5400` wrapper is gone. r6's mid leg ran **uncapped** and its own process
  tree peaked at **18.263 GiB** — twice the cap it was given. The leg was
  kernel-OOM-killed inside its own cgroup at 53.602 s with **no `evidence_id`**.
  **It licenses no id-stability conclusion whatsoever**; it licenses a *containment*
  conclusion only.
- **Reviewer finding (root cause, and the fix).** The drift did not originate in the
  leg; it originated in the **recipe**. r7's entry states its containment
  (`corpus/knowledge/2026-10-18-arc2-r7-pe-wall.md:13`: "9G memory cap + 1G swap cap"),
  and the wave followed it verbatim → bit-identical output. r6's entry states only the
  outcome (`…r6-pe-midrung.md:13`: exit 0, wall 2479 s, `analyzer_preset: ghidra-default`)
  — **no cap state, no memory profile** — so a rerun built from the entry silently
  changed the environment. The 18.263 GiB peak and the 10-decompile-child swarm live
  only in the round-6 staging record (`.ufo/waves/arc2-loop1-round6/E9.md:49-50`, `:66`).
  **Controlled-experiment discipline applied to ourselves: a recipe must carry its
  containment, not just its command.** §3 drafts the append-only fix.
