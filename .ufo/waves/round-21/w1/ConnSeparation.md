## Blocking conflicts and risks

- **Conditional test-harness separation conflict:** `battery.py:108-125` does not scope `--regression` to C2. Thus `--claim C1 --regression` loads the R21 C2 comparator and can make the overall C1 invocation depend on C2. The claims’ stated counting command omits this flag, so the prescribed C1-only command remains separable; nevertheless, do not use regression mode to substantiate C1 independence. If a future caller must combine them, the runner should reject that combination or route regression through a separate C2-only invocation.
- **C1 coverage gap:** the claim also includes removal of the old C3 receipt dependency, but the C1 12-case battery does not exercise that control. The separate scratch-root positive/decoy check above is necessary; merely seeing 12/12 case exits is insufficient for that clause.

A bare “C2” roll-up can misattribute the historical 19/19. - **Scope:** JSON record binding is local structural association, not authenticated origin. R21 C2’s zero/nonzero exits describe a bounded observation comparator, not successful target analysis; preserve the explicit pathless/phase-VA and historical-data limits.
