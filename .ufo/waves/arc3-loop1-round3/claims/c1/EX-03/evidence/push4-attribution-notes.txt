CONSEQUENCE FOR THE CHAIN:
  (a) PUSH4/PUSHn inventories must recover reduced constants by unsigned-shift
      arithmetic before matching against selectors or error selectors;
  (b) a linear sweep must exclude (or explicitly bound) the trailing metadata region;
  (c) the dispatch compare sites themselves push the PLAIN selector — measured: all 9
      dispatch sites in CarrierA and all 5 in OptVault carry the unreduced selector.
