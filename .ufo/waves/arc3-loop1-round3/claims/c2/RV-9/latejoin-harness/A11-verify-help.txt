usage: custody-pair.py verify [-h] [--live] [--require-paths N]
                              receipt_a [receipt_b]

positional arguments:
  receipt_a          reference receipt
  receipt_b          candidate receipt

options:
  -h, --help         show this help message and exit
  --live             compare receipt_a against the live filesystem
  --require-paths N  exit 2 (USAGE) when the reference carries fewer than N
                     paths (D3 coverage gate)
