           claims/c2/PL-2/candidate-with-s5.patch.md,
           claims/c2/PL-2/candidate-coupled-vyper.patch.md
           (all generated by claims/c2/PL-2/gen-and-measure.py)

MEASURED (VZ-4, 14:27Z):

1. The hunks carry a NON-STANDARD header. PL-2's own text shows the form:

     ```diff
     --- a/PROVINCE.md
     +++ b/PROVINCE.md
     @@ line 83 @@
     -  procedures via Ghidra). Reserve: 91MB Godot ARM64 template, ARM64X hybrids.
     +  procedures via Ghidra). The 91MB reserve is consumed; scale wall closed (arc2-r7/r8). ARM64X hybrids untested.
     ```

   `@@ line 83 @@` is not a valid unified-diff range header.

2. Standard `patch` REJECTS the file:
     $ patch -p1 --batch --forward --dry-run < pl2.patch
     patch: **** Only garbage was found in the patch input.

   (VZ-4 extracted all 22 fenced `diff` blocks and fed them as one patch — the same
   result. PL-2's own generator confirms the design: `apply_hunks(lines, hunks)` at
   gen-and-measure.py:190 applies (name, line, pattern, replacement, why) tuples
   against the ORIGINAL text, and its header states "hunks are byte-exact and
   content-anchored (line numbers informational)".)

3. PL-2 ships NO applier. The directory holds the three patch texts, the generator,
   and corpus-erratum-coupled.patch.md — no `apply.py` / `apply.sh`. A parent
   cannot land these with the standard tool; they require either PL-2's generator
   (which regenerates rather than applies) or a bespoke parser.

CONSEQUENCE: PL-2's candidate is the MOST COMPLETE content-wise (VZ-4 verified via a
bespoke parser: 22/22 hunks land, 162 lines, 163a546e…, census PASS, all seven stale
needles repaired), but it is the LEAST landable as-is. By contrast EX-11's
A+B+C+D, EX-13's per-row set, and CR-2's correction are all standard unified diffs
that `patch --fuzz=0` accepts cleanly (EX-14 v2 is standard but fuzz-dependent —
see c2-v2-fuzz-defect.txt).

RECOMMENDATION FOR THE PARENT: if PL-2's content is wanted (it is the only candidate
that also repairs the pacman clause AND carries the coupled-Vyper variant), either
(a) ask PL-2's generator for a standard unified-diff emission, or (b) apply the

files as written.

This is the same class as the EX-11 incident: an artifact whose stated form does not
match its actual form. Both were caught by re-deriving rather than trusting.
