=== TRAP 3 (new this round, first-hand) — Vyper offsets land AFTER JUMPI, not on JUMPDEST ===
VZ-4 probe of three independent on-host Vyper 0.4.3 runtimes, all hex legs, rea-agents@6.4.0:

  CarrierV (EX-04) 15/15   on JUMPDEST=0   immediately after JUMPI(0x57)=15   other=0
  CarrierW (PL-1)   9/9    on JUMPDEST=0   immediately after JUMPI(0x57)=9    other=0
  Smoke    (EX-02)  4/4    on JUMPDEST=0   immediately after JUMPI(0x57)=4    other=0

Sample: CarrierV 0xd0e30db0 off=36 op=0x60 prev=0x57 ; 0x3f4ba83a off=536 op=0x34 prev=0x57.

This DIFFERS from the solc carriers of r1/r3, where every probe offset was measured to
land ON a JUMPDEST (0x5b). The Vyper dispatcher jumps to the instruction AFTER the
JUMPI — the callee body itself, not a JUMPDEST landing pad. So the r3 entry's
"offsets land on JUMPDEST" reading does NOT generalize to Vyper, and a checker that
asserts JUMPDEST-ness would report 0/15 on a perfectly recovered Vyper carrier.

The correct Vyper-side structural reading is "the offset's preceding byte is JUMPI (0x57)"
— which holds 15/15, 9/9, 4/4 above. PL-1 independently measured the same shape
(claims/c1/PL-1/evidence/verify.txt: "offsets on JUMPDEST: 0/15 ... preceded by JUMPI(0x57): 15/15").
=> Landing note for the C1 entry: state the Vyper offset convention explicitly, or the
   entry's structural claim will read as a 0/15 failure on its own carriers.
