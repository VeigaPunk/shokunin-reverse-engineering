## 6. The Vyper 0.4.3 dispatcher (decoded from bytes)

Prologue, measured identical in shape on two independently authored carriers
(this one and EX-04's `CarrierV.vy`):

```
PUSH0 CALLDATALOAD PUSH1 0x01 SHR PUSH1 0x02 PUSH1 <N> DUP3 MOD
PUSH1 0x01 SHL PUSH2 <table_off> ADD PUSH1 <len> CODECOPY PUSH0 MLOAD JUMP
<N x 2-byte big-endian jump targets>            <- table; ends exactly at runtime end
```

- Slot for selector `s` is `int(s) % N`. Verified **22/22** here (N=22) and **15/15** on
  `CarrierV.vy` (N=13) — a different modulus, same rule.
- Each dispatch site is `JUMPDEST PUSH4 <sel4> ...`; all 22 selector `PUSH4` sites are
  preceded by `0x5b` (histogram `{'5b': 22}`).
- Of the 22 slots, **13 are occupied** (`[1, 5, 7, 9, 10, 12, 13, 15, 17, 18, 19, 20,
  21]`) and **9 are empty** (`[0, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 11, 14, 16]`); every occupied slot's
  target is a chain head (`5b 63`), every empty slot's target is the shared body at
  `0x0a33`. Re-verified rigorously: each of the 22 selectors is reachable by walking
  the chain from its own `sel % 22` slot — **22/22, no failures**.
- Selector-level split: 13 selectors sit at their chain's head (the slot target *is*
  their site) and 9 are reached one or more links deeper through
  `... DUP2 XOR PUSH2 <next> JUMPI`.
- Prologue bytes here: `5f3560e01c60026016820660011b610cdd01601e395f51565b63d0e30db081`.
  **It opens with the same `5f3560e01c` sequence the r3 entry recorded as the solc-0.8.37
  dispatcher anchor class** — so that anchor alone does not separate Vyper from solc.

## 7. Two traps in Vyper's `method_identifiers` (both reproduce)

**Trap 1 — leading-zero stripping.** `method_identifiers` prints selectors with the
leading zero nibbles removed. 3 of 23 entries here:

```
printed 0x6fdde03  -> real 0x06fdde03  name()
printed 0x95ea7b3  -> real 0x095ea7b3  approve(address,uint256)
printed 0xc751377  -> real 0x0c751377  fee_of(uint256,uint256)
```

Reproduces on `CarrierV.vy` (2/16 entries). A string compare against a zero-padded
4-byte set silently misses these.

**Trap 2 — a phantom `__default__()` entry.** `method_identifiers` emits
`"__default__()": "0x89402a72"`, a correct keccak of that literal (two independent
implementations agree), but the constant occurs **0 times in the runtime** and **0 times
in the creation blob**. The ABI instead carries `{"stateMutability": "payable",
"type": "fallback"}` with no selector. So `len(method_identifiers) = 23` while
`len(ABI functions) = 22`; the delta is exactly this entry. Reproduces on `CarrierV.vy`.

Consequence for a K/K probe: the correct ABI-derived K for a Vyper contract is the
**function** entries only. Taking `method_identifiers` as K injects one guaranteed false
positive per contract.

## 8. Attacks / refutations attempted

| # | attack | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | compile is nondeterministic | **refuted** — 3 independent runs, `runtime.hex` and `abi.json` byte-identical each time and equal to the landed artifacts |
| 2 | the local keccak is wrong | **refuted** — 3 independent implementations (pure-Python in the decoder, js-sha3@0.8.0 from the receipted r1 prefix, node:crypto/OpenSSL `keccak-256`) agree on all 22 signatures; self-test vectors `keccak256("")` and `keccak256("transfer(address,uint256)")` match Ethereum's own |
| 3 | the dispatch model is overfitted to one carrier | **refuted** — decoder generalizes to `CarrierV.vy` unchanged: N=13, 15/15, `sel % 13` holds 15/15 |
| 4 | every `PUSH4` constant is a selector | **refuted** — with a disassembly-aware sweep (PUSH immediates skipped) 24 distinct constants appear at instruction boundaries, of which only 22 are selectors. `0x08c379a0` is the real `Error(string)` selector (a plausible decoy); `0xc4a11628` is *not* an instruction-boundary constant at all — it is the middle of the `Transfer` topic `ddf252ad…f5a4df523b3ef` carried in a PUSH32. A boundary-blind scan sees 27 distinct constants and 5 false positives |
| 5 | Vyper carries a solc-style CBOR trailer in the runtime | **refuted** — `--no-bytecode-metadata` leaves the runtime byte-identical (3337 B both ways) and shortens only the creation blob by 55 B; the 55-byte trailer is `…a1657679706572830004030037` ("vyper" marker). No `a1657679706572` in the runtime at all |
| 6 | the seat-line requirement is met by one lucky function | **measured** — ABI reports 22 functions across 4 mutability classes, so payable/view/pure separation is exercised, not incidental |

## 10. Scope and limits

- Claims here are scoped to **vyper 0.4.3+commit.bff19ea2**, default codegen
  (`-O gas`, no `--experimental-codegen`), default EVM version, compiled on this host
  this round. Other Vyper versions, `-O codesize/none`, `--experimental-codegen`, and
  non-default `--evm-version` are **untested** — the prologue/trap results are not
  claimed to generalize to them.
- The dispatcher decode is a **static** model validated against the compiler's own ABI
  and against two sibling seats' independent reconstructions — not against execution:
  no EVM was run, no transaction was sent, no chain was queried. The `sel % N` rule is
  consistent with every selector on two carriers (N=22 and N=13) but is not proven for
  all inputs.
- No `inspect-evm-interface` run from this seat (out of scope by seat line); the K/K
  comparison here is between the compiler's ABI and this seat's own decoder. EX-06 and
  EX-08 supplied the probe legs; the probe evidence is theirs, not this seat's.
- The `5f3560e01c` prologue observation is about the *opening bytes only*; it does not
  claim Vyper and solc dispatchers are otherwise the same (they are not — §6).
- This seat wrote only its report and `claims/c1/EX-03/`; scratch under `/var/tmp/ex03-r4`.
