# R6 (reviewer) — meta-review: calibration or freshness?

**VERDICT: CONDITIONAL FAIL as currently specified.** C1 has the right calibration target—whether R1–R4 assessed their own outcomes accurately—but its current metric does not freeze a claim-level denominator or require auditing round verdicts/admission decisions. Without those constraints, a current-state census can masquerade as calibration and overlap C3 freshness. This is a measurement-design finding, not an audit of all R1–R4 claims.

Axes reviewed: **calibration fidelity** (prior self-assessments are correct), **freshness separation** (current missed-item debt remains C3), and **reproducibility** (historical result from immutable evidence, not today's mutable environment).

## Findings

1. **The boundary can be made clean.** Calibration asks: “Was each recorded before/after assertion and the resulting improved/unchanged/unprovable/not-verified/admitted decision true on its cited source revisions, using the evidence then recorded?” Freshness asks: “Which R1–R4 missed items remain actionable now, and which safe landings can happen now?” C1 should audit the former; C3 owns the latter. Re-running the v10 census on today's tree/config, counting today's stale pointers, or fixing those pointers is not evidence of historical calibration.

2. **The proposed denominator is not operational yet.** “N audited / confirmed / dispositioned” needs a frozen inventory of *distinct atomic assertions*, deduplicated across SCORECARD, LOG and the round reports. Split compound rows (for example, r1 freshness includes multiple measurements); count unchanged and negative claims too; include the round-level admission verdicts. Otherwise a seat could choose an easy subset, count only numeric deltas, or ignore an “honest stop”/false-green verdict while reporting a large N. A discrepancy is not confirmed merely because a later edit makes HEAD clean.

3. **Historical evidence must be commit-paired and input-pinned.** The v10 census reads its declared archive root but also reads live client configs and current npx caches: `.ufo/gates/c1-census-v10.py:5-10,74-80,124-134,218-234,376-399`. That is a current-surface/freshness instrument by design, not a historical self-assessment auditor. For each old assertion, C1 must use archives of the cited before/after commits and committed receipts/raw artifacts or frozen instrument inputs. If the historical result depended on an unpreserved live probe or volatile external input, classify it unresolved; do not silently substitute a present-day run.

4. **Endpoint agreement is not sufficient for custody claims.** My archive comparison found equal pre/post receipt hashes in rounds 1–3, but R3's own report records a witnessed in-window mutate-and-revert and voids the bare zero-drift claim (`.ufo/reports/arc2-loop1-round3/kaidoku.md:81-82`). Round 4's archived endpoints differ on Kimi config, and the report also records the in-window rewrite (`.ufo/reports/arc2-loop1-round4/kaidoku.md:79-81`). C1 must compare the *claim's exact scope* to the full contemporaneous captures/drift record, not equate “same endpoint hashes” with “no drift.”

5. **Round verdicts are themselves calibration claims.** R3 says “1 of 3 claims verified” and is not admitted (`.ufo/reports/arc2-loop1-round3/kaidoku.md:9-10,108-118`); R4 says all three verify and admits the round (`.ufo/reports/arc2-loop1-round4/kaidoku.md:10,107-117`). R4 R6 reports a concrete false-current-claim fixture that reaches census PASS under the declared re-key (`.ufo/waves/arc2-loop1-round4/R6.md:38-49`), plus a frozen-run guard that accepts hand-edited pointer tables when the census stays the same (`:25-37`). These recorded review findings must be adjudicated by C1; simply re-running the final census/probe and copying “PASS” would repeat the result, not calibrate its scope or reliability.

6. **Prior review records show why a per-assertion ledger matters.** Earlier R6 records found measurement-unit/command mismatches (r1: `.ufo/waves/arc2-loop1-round1/R6.md:18-24,88-93`), an undefined C1 count and competing 6-vs-11 readings (r2: `.ufo/waves/arc2-loop1-round2/R6.md:7-16,64-70`), and a mis-scoped pointer census/target (r3: `.ufo/waves/arc2-loop1-round3/R6.md:16-20,50-69`). These are recorded findings, not freshly remeasured by this seat. The later round records state narrower units and dispositions (r1 report `:113-123`; r3 report `:70-74`); C1 should verify those dispositions against the cited source archives rather than treat old findings as automatically open freshness debt.
