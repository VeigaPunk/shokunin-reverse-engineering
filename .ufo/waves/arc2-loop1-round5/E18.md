## Conclusions

- **Observation:** R2, R3, and R4 each record 64 dispatched, 60 reports delivered, and four non-completions; archive recount confirms 60 seat-report files in each recorded tree.
- **Observation:** R2's claims were verified, R3's round was not admitted, and R4's claims were verified/admitted. The dispatch ratio alone did not determine claim verdicts.
- **Inference:** Closeout needs two separate checks: logical-slot delivery/accounting and claim-specific evidence/adjudication. A 60/64 count is not itself a claim pass or failure.
- **Inference:** The delivered report files alone cannot identify which four logical slots were missing. Re-dispatch must use the parent's authoritative dispatch roster/receipts, not guessed seat IDs or filename sequence.
- **Unknown:** Why the same four-report deficit recurred; whether the same logical slots were absent each round; whether the parent has external dispatch records that identify them. Round 4 explicitly calls the cause uninvestigated.

```markdown
# Wave closeout when 60 of 64 reports arrive

## Trigger and evidence

Use this procedure whenever a wave declares 64 logical seats but closes with 60 delivered reports. In arc2-loop1 rounds 2, 3, and 4, each round recorded four non-completions; the defect recurred three consecutive times (`SCORECARD.md:710-712, 774-775, 858-861`). The reports establish the delivery shortfall, not its cause.

## Verification pass

1. Freeze the authoritative dispatch roster before counting: one row per logical seat, with its task/dispatch ID, requested assignment, batch, provider/model, and dispatch status. Record the source of this roster. A count of launch calls is not a count of delivered reports.
2. Enumerate the expected report paths and reconcile them against the roster. For every logical seat, classify exactly one state: valid report received; never dispatched; dispatched but no completed report; malformed/misrouted report; or duplicate/ambiguous output. Record the exact count and the command/output used to enumerate files.
3. Identify missing logical seats by set difference against the roster and dispatch receipts. Do not infer their IDs from the number 64, from gaps in filenames, or from the 60 reports alone. If the authoritative roster is unavailable, record the identities as unknown and retrieve the parent-side dispatch record before redispatch.
4. Validate each received report against its expected seat/task identity and assignment. Keep this delivery audit separate from the independent-evidence and acceptance checks for each claim.

## Re-dispatch rule

If 64 completed logical seats are required, re-dispatch exactly one replacement for each known unresolved logical seat—four replacements for a verified 60/64 closeout. Do not re-run or replace a completed seat merely to pad the count. Give each replacement a fresh attempt/seat ID, preserve the original logical-slot mapping, scope, constraints, and independence requirements, and use only an authorized dispatch route. Batch the known replacements together when the dispatch mechanism permits.

After replacement delivery, repeat the roster-to-report reconciliation and deduplicate by logical slot, not by number of attempts or files. If a replacement also fails, record the remaining deficit and stop claiming 64/64; any further retry needs an explicit parent/operator decision. If the mission permits a partial close, the parent may adjudicate claim evidence that independently meets its criteria, but must still report the wave as 60/64 (or its final reconciled count), state why no further backfill was done, and not waive the delivery defect silently.

A 60/64 wave is an operational shortfall, not an automatic claim verdict. R2's claims verified via convergence; R3 verified only 1/3 and was not admitted; R4 verified all three and was admitted (`SCORECARD.md:710-712, 772-775, 858-861`). Judge each claim by its own frozen evidence and acceptance criteria; neither promote nor reject a claim solely because four seats did not report.

## Closeout record form

- Wave / round ID and declared logical-seat count:
- Roster source (path/receipt) and enumeration command:
- Initial dispatch batches (provider/model, requested slots, accepted/started slots):
- Initial report count (unique valid logical slots / declared slots):
- Slot reconciliation (one row per unresolved slot): logical slot | dispatch evidence/status | missing/invalid report evidence | replacement attempt ID | final status:
- Replacement batch(es) (time, authorized route, slot-to-attempt mapping):
- Final unique completed logical slots / declared slots; duplicate or malformed outputs:
- Per-claim evidence/adjudication and whether each required threshold was met:
- Round admission decision and rationale, separate from delivery count:
- Dispatch root cause: confirmed evidence or `unknown`; owner/follow-up:
```
