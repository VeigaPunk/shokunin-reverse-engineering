## Conclusions

### Observation

- The eight command-A outputs above are re-derived from the named commit objects: coverage remains 79/79 and 10.0; the only craft-depth increases are round 1 (9→10) and round 4 (10→11).
- R1’s marker-aware result and raw text scan are distinct measures: the marker names `:60` and `:136`, while raw grep finds the added line `:141` too (`R6.md:58-93`).
- E22’s recorded baseline is `stale=5 ambiguous=1`; the round-2 scorecard/report measurement instead asserts stale 6 + contradicted 1 + ambiguous 1 (`E22.md:84-100`; `SCORECARD.md:741`).
- The R3 report explicitly defines the append-only measure as “unwarned” and says raw old lines remain (`E23.md:938-1004`). The R3 91.6MB run and scratch gate results are historical report evidence, not reproduced REA executions.
- The committed R4 probe selftest and valid revision pass; its invalid-revision run reports seven findings with exit 0. The R4 drift record explicitly voids config zero-drift.

### Inference

- R1’s defensible statement is “two previously unqualified loci became explicitly scoped,” not “raw matching lines fell 2→0.”
- R2’s pre-count must be corrected or its category definitions reconciled against E22; the printed `6/1/1` is not established by the cited baseline output.
- R3’s `6→0` is valid only when labeled **unwarned loci**. It must not be paraphrased as removal of six raw dead pointers from append-only files.
- R4’s “bad-input classes each exit 2” is too broad: an invalid revision is accepted and converted into ordinary findings. The committed probe must validate `rev` before interpreting missing `git show` results as citation findings. The 12- and 16-case scratch variants do not cure the committed behavior.
- R4’s numeric rule increase is valid; the rule-identity wording should say the ten pre-existing rule blocks are unchanged, with old block 10 shifted to position 11.

### Unknown

- The live Kimi/config/cache anchors behind R2’s v9 before/after and R4’s v10 census were not re-read or rerun; these are recorded outcomes, not fresh validations.
- R3’s REA coverage run and the R3 scratch gate prototypes cannot be reconstructed from the cited commit trees; their archive records support an honest stop/non-landing, not an independently verified tool result.
- Equal R1/R2 receipt endpoints do not alone prove every instant between captures. The historical reports cite in-window captures, but this audit did not inspect live config files or recreate that interval.
