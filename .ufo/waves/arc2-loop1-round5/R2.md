# R2 — C2 design review: 60/64 dispatch accounting

**Verdict: The 60/64 count is reproducible as a count of tracked seat-report files, not as proof that exactly 60 tasks completed or that the other four failed. The four unmatched report slots are E25–E28 in rounds 2–4; their task outcomes remain unknown.**

## Observations

- I re-counted the artifact commits, not the mutable current worktree. Round 1 has 64 top-level wave markdown files; rounds 2, 3, and 4 each have 60. Comparing each to the round-1 roster, the same four files are absent in every later round: `E25.md`, `E26.md`, `E27.md`, `E28.md`.
- The round reports say 64 seats were dispatched and 60 seat reports delivered, while labeling the difference “4 non-completions” (`.ufo/reports/arc2-loop1-round2/kaidoku.md:3-5`, round 3 `:3-5`, round 4 `:3-6`). The SCORECARD repeats this as a dispatch defect and explicitly says the root cause is uninvestigated (`SCORECARD.md:710-712,774-775,858-862`). These are records of the claim, not independent per-seat terminal evidence.
- Round 2’s closure note is more precise: the wave was “complete except 4 seat reports”; it says sessions were closed, but does not enumerate terminal outcomes for the four missing slots (`.ufo/reports/arc2-loop1-round2/kaidoku.md:114-116`).
- The earlier R8 review quotes the protocol rule “missing report → lane-round failed” (`.ufo/waves/arc2-loop1-round2/R8.md:203-209`). That is an artifact/admission rule for the lane-round; it does not establish that a particular seat task failed. R8 also notes the watcher is existence-only, not content validation (`.ufo/waves/arc2-loop1-round2/R8.md:121-132`).

## Inference

The current analysis is sound only when worded as “60 of 64 expected seat-report artifacts are present.” The supplied swarm-summary figure `completed:60` is a separate aggregate claim. Even if accepted as an authoritative completion count, it does not map the 60 completions to filenames or explain the other four. The repeated E25–E28 gap suggests a systematic dispatch/reporting boundary, but does not identify its cause.

## Unknown

The reviewed tracked records do not establish whether E25–E28 were never dispatched, remained incomplete, failed/cancelled, or completed without their reports being written/landed. The raw swarm summaries and per-seat terminal statuses were not available in the cited round artifacts, so I could not verify the meaning of `completed:60` or link its count to those four IDs. “Failed” and “completed-but-unwritten” are both unsupported classifications at seat level.

## Verbatim lesson text for parent

```text
Dispatch accounting keeps four measures separate: requested seats, confirmed task dispatches, per-task terminal outcomes, and report artifacts landed. Never infer task completion or failure from report-file existence or absence. At close, reconcile each stable seat/task ID against the dispatch record, terminal status, and report path; publish the four counts independently. If a report is absent but its task status is not evidenced, record `report_missing; task_status=unknown`—not `failed` and not `completed`. Redispatch only after checking the original task’s status, and record the original/replacement task IDs to avoid duplicate work. For arc2-loop1 rounds 2–4, re-counts at the artifact commits show 60 reports each, with E25–E28 absent each time; the aggregate `completed:60` does not resolve those four per-seat outcomes.
```
