## 4. Attacks / refutations attempted, with verdicts

1. **Null experiment (identical bytes), 1x/8x/32x × subprocess+in-process** —
   bounds the noise: ±0.7 ms (5 runs), ±0.2 ms (25 runs), ±0.18 ms (in-process,
   32x). Verdict: measured guard deltas ≤ null deltas; the strong form "the
   guards cost exactly zero" is *not* claimed, the frozen claim's weaker form
   ("no cost-class change") stands.
2. **Size artefact at 8x** (before→after +1.4 ms, ratio 1.0199): padded copy of
   `before` at exactly the landed byte size gives −0.089 ms, and the
   size-matched comparison gives −0.356 ms (sign flips). Verdict: refuted —
   the 8x gap is drift/compile-order noise, not guard work.
3. **Ordering bias** (harness always runs A first): reversed order gives
   −0.123 ms. Verdict: no systematic bias.
4. **"You timed your own reconstruction, not the real artifact"** — re-measured
   against the landed revision and three later ones. Verdict: addressed; the
   verdict is identical across all five revisions.
5. **"A FAILing instrument may cost differently"** — guard-fired paths measured
   at 1x and 32x. Verdict: time-neutral.
6. **Anchor confound** — the census re-derives live pins from the three custody
   configs each run; receipts show identical anchors in every run
   (`devin 6.5.0 / omp 6.5.0 / kimi 6.7.0`), reads read-only. Verdict: no
   confound.
7. **Semantic attack on a mid-flight revision (most consequential finding)** —
   revision `c95a5cf3` introduced a per-readable-file floor counted over
   block-level current-ok records; at HEAD those records are PROVINCE.md=1,
   SCORECARD.md=4, SKILL.md=0 (SKILL.md's only locus-bearing block is mixed,
   disposition `info`), so an **ordinary HEAD run returned
   `DECLARED FAIL CLASSES: ['BLIND-INSTRUMENT']`, `VERDICT: FAIL`, exit 1** —
   contradicting the frozen C2 clause "ordinary runs at HEAD stay PASS 0/0/0/0".
   Receipts `receipts/after-c95a5cf3-head-verbose.out` / `.json`. The next
   revision `df9ffdf9` counts current-ok *detections* (PROVINCE 17 / SCORECARD
   14 / SKILL 3) and restores HEAD-PASS; `a4c16763` and the newest `d0772ce8`
   still PASS. Verdict: the mid-flight revision violated the clause (live
   ~1 min, fixed); the clause needs re-checking on whatever revision lands last.
