usage: custody-pair.py [-h] {capture,verify,selftest,paths} ...

v2 custody receipt instrument (capture/verify/selftest) for the 5 frozen
paths.

positional arguments:
  {capture,verify,selftest,paths}
    capture             write a v2 receipt (read-only over the paths)
    verify              compare two receipts, or a receipt against the live
                        filesystem
    selftest            fixture-based selftest (never touches a custody path)
    paths               print the 5 canonical custody paths

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
