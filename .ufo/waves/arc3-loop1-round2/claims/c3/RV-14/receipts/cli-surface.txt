=== custody-pair.py  --help ===
usage: custody-pair.py [-h] {capture,verify,selftest,paths} ...

v2 custody receipt instrument (capture/verify/selftest) for the 5 frozen
paths.

positional arguments:
  {capture,verify,selftest,paths}
    capture             write a v2 receipt (read-only over the paths)
    verify              compare two receipts, or a receipt against the live
                        filesystem
    selftest            fixture-based selftest (never touches a custody path)
    paths               print the 5 canonical custody paths

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
rc=0
=== custody-pair.py capture --help ===
usage: custody-pair.py capture [-h] --out OUT [--label LABEL]
                               [--paths [PATHS ...]]

options:
  -h, --help           show this help message and exit
  --out OUT            receipt path, or '-' for stdout
  --label LABEL        capture label recorded in the header
  --paths [PATHS ...]  override paths (default: the 5 custody paths)
rc=0
=== custody-pair.py verify --help ===

positional arguments:
  receipt_a   reference receipt

options:
  -h, --help  show this help message and exit
  --live      compare receipt_a against the live filesystem
