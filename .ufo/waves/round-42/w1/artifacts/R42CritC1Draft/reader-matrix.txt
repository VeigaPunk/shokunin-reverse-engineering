- unsquashfs 4.7.5 reads v1–v3 gzip only; LZMA v3 (the dominant real-world flavor:

  for LZMA v3 carriers.

- Therefore the draft's pair {unsquashfs 4.7.5, 7-Zip} satisfies "agree file-for-file"
  only on gzip v3 images; on LZMA v3 images the pair cannot even both read the carrier.
