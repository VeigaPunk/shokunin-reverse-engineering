## CLAIM
The r4 “three-lane” basis comprises three different evidence strands, not three independent tests proving every evidence-id family hashes paths.

## METHOD
Read-only `git grep -n` and `git show HEAD:<path>` over the kaidoku corpus; no REA/MCP/network probes. Source pointers below are HEAD object paths and lines.

## FINDINGS
- `corpus/QUALITY.md:24` names the three strands: (1) revenger verifier, same bytes/different paths; (2) Android `jadx` per-run IDs versus `apktool` deterministic IDs; (3) `trace-application-feature` same-path determinism. The r4 erratum is duplicated at `corpus/knowledge/2026-10-13-r2-evidence-bundle-schema.md:244-249` and `corpus/knowledge/2026-10-13-r3-mcp-session.md:152-157`; the lesson summarizes it at `corpus/lessons/2026-10-13-round-4.md:11-15`.
- Revenger is the only strand explicitly described as same-content/different-path, citing `ev_8efa2368` → `ev_50038b96` (`corpus/lessons/2026-10-13-round-4.md:14-15`). Its verifier/run record is not present in this repo’s HEAD, so underlying details are unverifiable here.
- Android evidence is narrower: three identical-input `inspect-android-package` runs yielded three IDs because `raw_result` included per-run mkdtemp paths and `load_duration_ms`, while `normalized_result` stayed identical (`corpus/knowledge/2026-10-13-r4u-02-jadx-decode.md:137-149`). `decode-android-resources` reproduced its ID on identical invocation (`:48-70`). This compares two operations; it does not test changed paths.
- The trace strand is asserted by the r4 summary/erratum, but r3’s recorded `trace-application-feature` calls are one each for different seeds, with no repeated-run output (`corpus/knowledge/2026-10-13-r3-renderer-asar.md:71-73,101-102`); same-path rerun evidence is unverifiable from the corpus.
- R4 itself records a family exception: `analyze_function` uses `f(content, params)`; renamed-path dossiers retained the same ID despite a different `subject` (`corpus/knowledge/2026-10-13-r4-16-frontier-rescan.md:427-433`).

## THREATS
Corpus-only review; no live probes by assignment. The cited strands vary in verb family and test design, so they do not justify a universal path-sensitive formula. The underlying revenger and trace rerun artifacts are unverifiable in this HEAD.

## VERDICT
FINDING: r4’s three strands are revenger cross-path, Android per-run/stable comparison, and trace same-path determinism; only the first is explicitly a cross-path test.
