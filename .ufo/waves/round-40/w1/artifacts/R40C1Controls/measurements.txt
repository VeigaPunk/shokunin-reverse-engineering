## 7. What was not run, and why

* **No REA/unblob on any C1 carrier** (V1/V2 members, lane-built AVM carriers, the round-11 body):
  bounds. The carrier controls are evaluated with committed-bytes and pure-Python reads, and the
  failure shapes they need were measured on dev inputs (marker-only body, K1 copy).
* **The rerun clause is not executed** by this seat: it needs the frozen carriers. Its mechanism is
  measured with two honest REA runs of a dev carrier plus the projection comparison; the suite run
  reports `rerun_projection_ok: null` for that reason, and CTL-C1-2's `expected_byte_only` records
  the measured byte-only verdict (`met_target true`).
* **The honest round-40 tree does not exist yet**, so the suite is validated on a dev fixture. The
  freeze must re-run `run-controls.sh` against the real tree and paste the 18 lines into the score
  receipt.

No tool was installed or upgraded (no pacman transaction; no uv/npx install beyond the pinned
`npx -y rea-agents@6.5.0` the bounds prescribe for REA). `sasquatch`, `sasquatch-v4be` and `unblob`
were read and executed as installed, never reinstalled or moved. Read-only git only in the lane;
the fixture repo lives entirely under scratch.
